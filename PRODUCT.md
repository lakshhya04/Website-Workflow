# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Caffeine-sensitive drinkers who still care about flavour. They want a cup that tastes excellent in the afternoon or late evening without costing them sleep.

## Product Purpose

Vesper sells specialty-grade decaf coffee. The site's job is to get a visitor to the shop ("Shop the roast"). Success is a visitor who believes decaf can be a first-class cup and goes to buy it.

## Positioning

Flavour-first decaf. Tasting notes and roast quality lead, and the process claims (Swiss Water Process, small-batch roasting) back them up.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single static page built as plain HTML and CSS (`index.html`, `styles.css`), with all visual values coming from `design/tokens.css`.
- The shop link currently points to `#`. Where the shop lives is undecided.
- Dark-mode colours in `design/tokens.css` still hold the earlier green palette and have not been redone for Vesper. Undecided.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Vesper. Tagline: "Decaf, done properly."
- The current copy was approved verbatim by the owner. Change headline, lead, button, proof points or footer only when asked.
- Spelling follows the approved copy (British, for example "flavour").

## Evidence on Hand

- Product claims in the approved copy: Swiss Water Process, 99.9% caffeine-free, no chemical solvents; single-origin beans, small-batch roasting, shipped within seven days; tasting notes of cocoa, dried fig and a clean finish.
- There are no real reviews, press, customer counts or awards yet. Future work must not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with flavour. Process claims support the cup and never replace it.
2. Speak to people who avoid caffeine without making them feel they are settling.
3. State only claims the owner has confirmed.
4. One clear action: shop.
